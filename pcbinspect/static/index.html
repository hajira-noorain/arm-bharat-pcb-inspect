How small a defect can this station find?

Like an eye chart: a printed card has defects that get smaller, each size repeated 30 times. The station counts how many it finds and states its own limit for this camera, lighting and software. Repeat it after changing any of them.

  1. Print the card at 100% (actual size) and check the 100 mm bar with a ruler.
  2. Put sheet 0, the defect-free sheet, under the camera at the same height you inspect boards, and press Capture.
  3. Capture each numbered sheet the same way, in any order.
  4. Press Read the card.

Add a customer requirement

Paste one acceptance requirement as the customer wrote it. Llama on this station and a keyword reader each turn it into a rule; you confirm before it is used. The station also says whether it can actually guarantee the rule, from its last eye test.

Rules in use

They decide what happens to the defect types they mention. Everything else follows the standard IPC-style table.

Teach a new board design

Put good boards of the new design under the camera one after another. The station learns what good looks like from them, with no training run.

Learn from your corrections

Every time you confirm a defect or mark a false alarm, the station keeps an example. Updating the model uses them, but the new version is only switched on if it hides no real defects and does better than before.
