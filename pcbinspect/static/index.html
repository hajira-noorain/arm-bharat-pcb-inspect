Teach a new board design

Show the station good boards of the new design. It learns what good looks like from them, with no training run.

  1. Name the design

  2. Capture good boards

    Put a good board against the corner stops with the printed 20 mm marker beside it, and capture. Lift it, put it down again (or use another good board) and capture again. Three or more captures work best.

  3. Finish

    The station draws test defects of known size onto your pictures to set its own pass and fail thresholds. This takes a few minutes.

  4. Capture good boards and learn

Learn from your corrections

Every confirmed defect and false alarm is kept as an example. Updating the model uses them, but the new version is switched on only if it hides no real defects and does better than before.

How small a defect can this station find?

Like an eye chart: a printed card has defects that get smaller, each size repeated 30 times. The station counts how many it finds and states its own limit for this camera, lighting and software.

  1. Print the card

    Download the card, print it at 100% and check the 100 mm bar with a ruler.

  2. Photograph every sheet

    Sheet 0 (defect-free) first, then each numbered sheet, at the height you inspect boards.

  3. Read the card

Add a customer requirement

Paste one acceptance requirement as the customer wrote it. Llama on this station and a keyword reader each turn it into a rule; you confirm before it is used. The station also says whether it can guarantee the rule, from its last eye test.
